Why Google Reviews Matter for Your Business

  • Local Ranking Power: They decide your local ranking. Businesses with more recent, positive reviews show up higher in Google Maps and the local pack.
  • The New Word of Mouth: Most people read reviews before visiting a restaurant, booking a salon, or calling a clinic.
  • Proof of Life: A steady stream of fresh reviews tells Google and customers that your business is active and reliable.
  • Free Customer Feedback: Reviews show you exactly what customers love — and what to fix next.

Pro Tips for Getting More Reviews

  • Ask at the Right Moment: Right after a positive interaction, when the customer is happiest — not days later.
  • Make it Effortless: Use the NFC smart card instead of asking customers to search for your business on Google Maps.
  • Personalize the Request: Mention their visit or order, and explain that authentic reviews genuinely support your business.
  • Reply to Every Review: A thoughtful reply shows you care — and prospective customers read your replies too.
  • Never Buy Reviews: Incentives violate Google policies. Great service plus a frictionless tap works far better.
